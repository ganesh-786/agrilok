# Support

## Before you ask

**agrilok is not a working product yet.** It is at Phase 0 (scaffolding). If
you came here looking for a study tool, there is nothing to use today. Watch
the repository or check [docs/roadmap.md](docs/roadmap.md) for progress.

**We are not the Public Service Commission.** We cannot tell you when a vacancy
will open, whether you are eligible, or what your result is. For those, go to
the federal PSC (psc.gov.np) or your Provincial PSC directly. See
[NOTICE](NOTICE).

## Where to go

| What you need | Where |
|---|---|
| Something in the study content is **wrong** | Open a [Content error issue](https://github.com/ganesh-786/agrilok/issues/new?template=content_error.yml) — highest priority we have |
| A bug in the app or tooling | Open a [Bug report](https://github.com/ganesh-786/agrilok/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) |
| An official source we should ingest | Open a [Source request](https://github.com/ganesh-786/agrilok/issues/new?template=source_request.yml) |
| An idea for a feature | Open a [Feature request](https://github.com/ganesh-786/agrilok/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml) |
| A question about how something works | [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/ganesh-786/agrilok/discussions) |
| A security or privacy problem | **Privately** — see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not open a public issue. |
| A copyright or takedown request | ganeshchaudhary4400@gmail.com — these are honoured first and discussed after |

## Language

Nepali and English are both fine. Write in whichever you are comfortable with.

नेपाली वा अंग्रेजी दुवै भाषामा लेख्न सक्नुहुन्छ।

## Response times

This is a volunteer project maintained in spare time. Content-error reports are
triaged first, because a wrong fact can cost someone an exam cycle. Everything
else is best-effort.

## Wrong content: what makes a good report

The more of this you can give, the faster it gets fixed:

- Where you saw it (page, topic, question).
- What it says.
- What it should say.
- The official document that proves it — a link to psc.gov.np, a Provincial PSC
  site, or another official source, if you have one.

You do not need the official link to file the report. Tell us it looks wrong and
we will check.
